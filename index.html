Power Calculator

AC Single Phase Power Formula:
• Apparent Power (S):
kVA = (V × I) / 1000

• Real Power (P):
kW = kVA × cos θ

• Reactive Power (Q):
kVAR = √(S² - P²)

Calculation Logic Reference:
1. Single Pulse Constant Duration (s):
t_s = (3600 × 1000) / (3 × V × I × PF × Pulse Rate)

N = ⌈T_target / t_s⌉

3. Actual Test Time (s):
T_act = N × t_s
